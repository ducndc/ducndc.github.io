Detailed Description

channel scan driver background configuration parameters.

Parameters
passive_dwell_timetime to wait on the channel during passive scans, in ms.
active_dwell_timetime to wait on the channel during active scans, in ms.
num_probe_reqsnumber of probe requests to send for the same SSID.
probe_reqs_intervaltime in ms, after which to fire the next round of probe requests for the same SSIDs.
num_chans_in_chunknumber of channels in single scan chunk.
break_timetime in ms, background scan break time duration for dfs channels.
break_time_busybusy flag for backround scan break.
window_sliceif passive_dwell_time is bigger than cts-to-self max time (32ms) Than we cut the scan into slices, this is time of each slice.
window_slice_overlapoverlapping of slices, the slices meant to cover full beacon interval time, to catch all networks, so we this is the time we of beacon interval 2 slices will be on.
